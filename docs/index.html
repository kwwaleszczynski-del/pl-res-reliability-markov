Wind and PV adequacy in Poland

Multi-state Markov models of wind and PV availability fitted to ERA5 at 29 grid points (), sequential Monte Carlo LOLE/EENS on the IEEE RTS-79 generating system with the RTS-79 and real KSE load (PSE), and ELCC by LOLE equality compared with the capacity-market derating factors (KWD). A regime-switching weather model with multi-day lulls drives the storage study at the bottom.

Storage and multi-day lulls

A first-order chain forgets a calm within a day or two. The regime model adds daily wind regimes with empirical run lengths (semi-Markov), shared by wind, PV and every grid point, optionally conditioned on the temperature of the weather year; storage then runs inside the sequential Monte Carlo.

Wind lulls: weather models against ERA5

Lulls per year lasting at least the given number of days; 200 synthetic years per model, ten ERA5 years for the data (national portfolio, equal capacity at 29 points).
