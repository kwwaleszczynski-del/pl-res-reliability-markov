Wind and PV adequacy in Poland

Multi-state Markov models of wind and PV availability fitted to ERA5 at 29 grid points (), sequential Monte Carlo LOLE/EENS on the IEEE RTS-79 generating system with the RTS-79 and real KSE load (PSE), and ELCC by LOLE equality compared with the capacity-market derating factors (KWD).
